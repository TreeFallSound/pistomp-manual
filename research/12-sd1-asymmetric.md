# SD-1 Asymmetric Clipping Research — Final Report

## Summary / Ranking

**No installed plugin is a faithful model of the Boss SD-1's specific asymmetric diode-feedback overdrive.** The genuine SD-1 (op-amp feedback loop with 2 diodes one way, 1 the other, soft clip, ~720 Hz mid-hump, ~1:2 clip-threshold ratio) is **not modeled by any plugin found on the pi-Stomp image**. The plugin literally named after it, `gx_sd1sim.lv2` (GxSD1), is the worst impostor — it uses a single odd-symmetric table via `copysign(f, -x)`; the diode asymmetry is not modeled at all (prior agent #3's finding is confirmed here at `GxPlugins.lv2/GxSD1.lv2/dsp/sd1sim.cc:144`).

What **does** exist is genuine asymmetric diode-clipping, just in the **wrong pedal families** (DS-1, RAT, Klon-ish Lovepedal, BaJa Tube Driver). The closest-to-SD-1 ranking is below, then a per-plugin paragraph for everything investigated.

## Per-plugin characterisation

### GxPlugins.lv2 (brummer10 standalone LV2s)

**`gx_sd1sim.lv2` (GxSD1 "Super Overdrive")** — **SYMMETRIC, IMPOSTOR.** Single `sd1table`, `sd1nonlin(x)` returns `copysign(f, -x)` (`GxSD1.lv2/dsp/sd1sim.cc:144`). Despite the name, it does NOT model the SD-1's diode asymmetry — it's effectively a TS-9-style odd-symmetric feedback soft clip with a different drive taper (confirmed by agent #3). Mid-hump, decent chord clarity, fine as a generic TS-style overdrive, useless if you specifically want SD-1 even-harmonic edge.

**`gx_bajatubedriver.lv2` (GxBaJaTubeDriver)** — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC.** Dual tables `bajatubedriver_table` (pos saturates ~0.412) and `bajatubedriver_neg_table` (neg ~-0.833); ratio **1:2.02**, almost exactly the SD-1's diode ratio. Tables generated by `DK/circ_table_gen.py` (circuit-simulator-derived). Used in a feedback arrangement (`bajatubedriver.cc:353`, three clipper calls summed minus a feedback term). Tube-driver circuit family (valve-style soft clip), mid-focused. This is the **best asymmetry-ratio match for the SD-1** in the entire set, but the underlying circuit is a tube driver, not a diode-feedback overdrive. Quality: high (precomputed circuit-sim tables). Tone: warm, sustain-y, mid-humped. Best use: standalone dirt or stack-boost before a cleaner amp.

**`gx_eternitysim.lv2` (GxEternity / Lovepedal Eternity)** — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC.** `signbit(fTemp3) ? lovepedal_negclip : lovepedalclip` (`eternity.cc:180`). Pos saturates ~0.458, neg ~-0.796, ratio **1:1.74**. Tables from `DK/circ_table_gen.py` (circuit-sim-derived). Op-amp feedback soft clip, Klon-adjacent "transparent overdrive" family. Quality: high. Tone: low-compression transparent overdrive, slight mid presence, retains clarity under gain — explicitly marketed as "natural sounding overdrive". Best use: standalone dirt or clean-ish boost into a slightly-broken amp. The **closest in circuit family** to the SD-1 (op-amp feedback soft clip + mid character), but it's a Lovepedal, not an SD-1, and the asymmetry ratio is the inverse of the SD-1's.

**`gx_timray.lv2` (GxTimRay / Jan Ray)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Has two tables (`janray_table`, `janray_neg_table`) but they're exact mirror images (neg = -pos). The Timmy/JAN Ray is a symmetric-clip pedal in real life, so this is faithful, but it's not an SD-1 substitute. Transparent overdrive, flat-ish tone, good chord clarity. Best use: standalone dirt.

**`gx_clubdrive.lv2` (GxClubDrive / Clubman)** — **MILDLY ASYMMETRIC.** Dual `clubman_p2_table` / `_neg_table` selected via `signbit` (`clubdrive.cc:250`). Same saturation magnitude (~±0.833) but different early slopes (pos -0.0116 vs neg +0.0169, ~1.45:1 in linear region). Op-amp feedback soft clip, low-gain overdrive family. Quality: high (circuit-sim tables). Tone: low-gain, warm, slightly compressed. Best use: stack-boost or low-gain rhythm.

**`gx_dop250.lv2` (GxDOP250 / DOD 250)** — **SYMMETRIC, IMPOSTOR.** Single 1N4148 `diode_table`, `diodeclip(x)` returns `copysign(f, x)` (`diode_table.h:59`). Despite the DOD 250 being asymmetric in real life (op-amp hard-clip with one diode to ground + one in feedback), this model is symmetric. Hard-clip-to-ground family. Bright, raspy, scooped-ish. Best use: standalone distortion, not a TS/SD stacker.

**`gx_mxrdist.lv2` (MXR Distortion+, in guitarix LV2 set)** — **SYMMETRIC.** The `.dsp` defines `clip = asymclip(_)` (asymmetric) but the `process` doesn't use it — the generated `mxrdist.cc` only calls `opamp2()` (single symmetric table 8, `copysign(f, -x)`). The asymclip is **dead code**. Real MXR Distortion+ is symmetric anyway (single diode pair clip to ground). Hard-clip distortion, bright, fizzy, thin. Best use: classic raspy standalone dirt.

**`gx_sd2lead.lv2` (GxSD2Lead)** — **SYMMETRIC.** `sd1nonlin` uses `copysign(f, -x)` with a single `sd1table` (and a `sd2table` for a second stage), all odd-symmetric. Despite the "SD2" name, no asymmetry. Boss SD-2 was a dual-channel pedal; this models it as two symmetric soft-clip stages. Best use: lead voice with two gain stages.

**`gx_guvnor.lv2` (GxGuvnor / Marshall Guv'nor)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `clip` table, `copysign(f, -x)` (`guvnor.cc:1465`). Marshall Guv'nor family — op-amp feedback soft clip, Marshall-flavoured mid-hump. Tone: middy, crunchy, good for rhythm chunk. Best use: standalone dirt for Marshall-style chunk.

**`gx_hotbox.lv2` (GxHotBox / Hughes&Kettner Tube Factor)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `nolintable` (sic — typo for "nonlinear"), `fabs(x)` based. Tube-factor-style soft clip. Mid-focused, smooth. Best use: low-gain boost or light dirt.

**`gx_epic.lv2` (GxEpic)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `epic_table`, `copysign(f, x)`. Op-amp boost/drive. Best use: clean-ish boost.

**`gx_liquiddrive.lv2` (GxLiquidDrive)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `clip1` table, `fabs(x)` based. Smooth liquid overdrive. Best use: lead sustain.

**`gx_luna.lv2` (GxLuna / Clay Jones OD)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `clayjonesod_table`, `copysign(f, x)`. TS-style clone, mid-hump. Best use: TS-family alternative.

**`gx_sloopyblue.lv2` (GxSloopyBlue / Bluesbreaker)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `bluesbreaker_clip_table`, `copysign(f, x)`. Marshall Bluesbreaker family — low-gain transparent overdrive. Best use: low-gain stack boost.

**`gx_valvecaster.lv2` (GxValveCaster)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `valvecaster_table`, `copysign(f, x)`. Valve-caster tube-overdrive-style. Best use: low-gain warmth.

**`gx_blueamp.lv2` (GxBlueAmp / Sarris Blues Amp)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `sarrisbluesamp_table`, `copysign(f, x)`. Best use: clean-ish amp-in-a-box.

**`gx_boobtube.lv2` (GxBoobTube)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `boobtube_table`, `copysign(f, x)`. Tube preamp model. Best use: low-gain warmth.

**`gx_shakatube.lv2` (GxShakaTube)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `shakatubep2_table`, `copysign(f, x)`. Tube-driver-style. Best use: smooth lead.

**`gx_heathkit.lv2` (GxHeathkit)** — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC.** Uses `exp(4*fTemp1)` for positive and `exp(4.8*(-fTemp1))` for negative (`Heathkit.cc:191-1912`) — a generalized tanh with different exponents per half. Asymmetry ratio ~4:4.8 ≈ 1:1.2. Tube preamp model (not diode). Quality: analytical closed-form, not circuit-sim. Tone: warm tube preamp grind. Best use: amp-like dirt. **Wrong family** (tube preamp, not diode pedal) but real asymmetry.

**`gx_saturate.lv2` (GxSaturator)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Multi-band Chebyshev-style saturator, `fabs`-based odd-symmetric shaping. Best use: colour/saturation, not dirt.

**`gx_tonemachine.lv2` (GxFoxx Tone Machine)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `clip` table, `copysign(f, x)`. Fuzz family (Foxx Tone Machine octave fuzz). Best use: colour/fuzz.

**`gx_microamp.lv2` (GxMicroAmp)** — Clean boost, no clipping (just `exp(7*(1-drive))` gain). Best use: clean boost.

**`gx_plexi.lv2` (GxPlexi)** — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC (tube).** Multiple asymmetric tables (`plexipoweramp_pre_table`/`_neg_table`, `plexipoweramp_table`/`_neg_table`, `plexipoweramptone_table`/`_neg_table`), all selected via `signbit`. Plexi full-amp model. Asymmetry is tube-power-amp, not diode. Best use: amp model, not pedal. Wrong family.

**`gx_uvox720k.lv2` (GxUVox720k / Vox UL730)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `clip` table, `copysign(f, x)`. Vox amp preamp model. Best use: amp-in-a-box.

**`gx_vmk2.lv2` (GxVmk2 / Vox Mk2)** — Multi-effect (driver + tremolo + reverb + vibe), the `driver` uses the same family. Symmetric per the pattern.

**`gx_saturator.lv2` (GxSat) / `gx_axisface.lv2` / `gx_creammachine.lv2` / `gx_tubedistortion.lv2`** — All single-table symmetric (`copysign` / `symclip`). Tube-preamp/12AX7 family.

### guitarix internal LV2 plugins (`guitarix/trunk/src/LV2/`)

**`gx_bossds1.lv2` (Boss DS-1)** — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC, BEST DIODE-MODEL MATCH.** Two asymmetric clip stages: `asymclip3` (soft clip, tables 6/7: pos 0.795 / neg 0.697, ratio 1.14:1) in the drive stage, and `asymhardclip2` (hard clip, tables 2/3: pos 0.801 / neg 0.675, ratio 1.19:1) after the op-amp (`bossds1.cc:246, 256`). Tables generated by `diode_table_gen.py` from SPICE-style diode equations. The DS-1's real asymmetry is mild (2 diodes to ground in one direction, 1 in the other → ~1.2:1), and this faithfully renders it. **This is the wrong pedal for an SD-1 substitute** (DS-1 is hard-clip-to-ground, not feedback soft clip; DS-1 has a fixed mid-hump around 1 kHz and is brighter/edgier than the SD-1's smoother 720 Hz hump), but it is the only plugin in the set with a genuine diode-equation asymmetric soft+hard clipper modelled from a real pedal. Quality: high (circuit-sim tables, Faust from a measured circuit). Best use: standalone distortion, stacks well before a clean amp. **If you want "the SD-1's even-harmonic harder feel" and the SD-1 sim is an impostor, the DS-1 is the closest available proxy** — same manufacturer's family, real diode asymmetry, just a different topology.

**`gx_aclipper.lv2` (ProCo RAT)** — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC.** Uses `asymhardclip2` (LV2 build) / `asymclip3` (internal) — asymmetric tables. Op-amp feedback + hard clip. RAT family: scooped, raspy, very high gain available. Quality: high (circuit-sim tables). Best use: standalone heavy distortion, NOT a stacker.

**`gx_scream.lv2` (Screaming Bird)** — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC but cheap.** Static `min(0.4514, max(-0.2514, x))` (scream.cc:95), ratio 1.8:1. Hand-coded static asymmetric hard clip (the diode-based `asymclip` is commented out). EHX Screaming Bird treble-booster/fuzz family. Best use: treble-boost into a fuzz or hot amp.

**`gx_hornet.lv2` (Hornet)** — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC but cheap.** Static `0.4*min(0.7514, max(-0.4514, x))` (hornet.dsp:3701), ratio 1.67:1. Fuzz face variant. Best use: fuzz.

**`gx_cstb.lv2`, `gx_fumaster.lv2`, `gx_fuzzface.lv2`, `gx_fuzzfacefm.lv2`** — Use `tranystage(TB_7199P_68k, ...)` — a single-ended BJT transistor stage model from `trany.lib`/`trany.h`. A single-ended transistor stage is **inherently asymmetric** (the transistor's transfer curve is nonlinear over the full swing, producing even harmonics), but the asymmetry comes from the **physics of the circuit topology**, not from a half-wave table swap. This is fuzz-family asymmetry, not diode-feedback asymmetry. Quality: medium (table-based transistor model). Best use: fuzz colour.

**`gx_muff.lv2` (Big Muff)** — Uses `symclip` (symmetric) in the LV2 build. Big Muff Pi family — actually uses a multi-stage transistor clipper in real life; the LV2 model is symmetric. Best use: sustain-y fuzz.

**`gx_bmp.lv2` (Big Muff Pi)** — Uses `symclip` — **SYMMETRIC** (`bmp.cc:220-221`). Same as above.

**`gx_hfb.lv2`, `gx_hogsfoot.lv2`, `gx_mole.lv2`, `gx_rangem.lv2`** — **No clipping stage at all** — pure filter/booster pedals (EHX HatDead Booster, Hogsfoot, Mole, Dallas Rangemaster treble booster). Symmetric by virtue of being linear. Best use: clean/treble boost into a dirt pedal or amp.

**`gx_jcm800pre.lv2`, `gx_studiopre.lv2`** — Tube preamp models using 12AX7/Alembic circuit tables. The `_neg_table` files exist but the pos/neg data is nearly mirror-image (numerical-solver noise only) → **effectively symmetric**. Tube preamp family. Best use: amp preamp.

**`gx_overdrive.lv2`** — `overdrive(x) = x*(|x|+drive)/(x² + (drive-1)|x| + 1)` — odd-symmetric (uses `abs(x)`). Generic soft overdrive. Best use: light dirt.

**`gx_distortion.lv2` / `gx_mbdistortion.lv2`** — Multi-band distortion using `valve.vt` (Steve Harrison's SWH Ladspa valve, applied symmetrically via `abs(x) : vt : neg : vt : neg : copysign`). **SYMMETRIC.** Best use: multi-band dirt colour.

### Non-guitarix plugins

**`ChowCentaur.lv2` (ChowCentaur)** — **SYMMETRIC.** Faithful Wave Digital Filter model of the Klon Centaur's clipping stage (`ClippingStage.h:62`): a single `DiodePair` with `Is=15e-6, Vt=0.02585` for both halves. The Klon's feedback clipper is actually a symmetric anti-parallel diode pair (two 1N34A germaniums), so this is **correct**, not a bug. Quality: very high (proper WDF diode-equation solver per Werner et al.). Tone: transparent, "money shot" mid-hump, Klon grain. Best use: standalone transparent overdrive or stack-boost before a slightly-broken amp. Not an SD-1 substitute — different family and symmetric.

**`OJD.lv2` (Schrammel OJD)** — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC but static.** The Waveshaper (`Waveshaper.h`) is a hand-drawn piecewise function: negative soft-clips between -1.7 and -0.3; positive between 0.9 and 1.1; hard clip at ±1.0. Asymmetry ratio ~1.5:1 (negative side clips earlier and softer). It's a **static waveshaper**, not a diode-equation model — the asymmetry is hand-coded, not derived from a circuit. JHS Bonsai/Pink Queen family (which has an SD-1 mode among others), with a pre-drive biquad boost (~1.6 kHz, Q~0.15) and post-drive tone shaping. Quality: medium (static waveshaper with 16× oversampling). Tone: middy, Marshall-flavoured, good chord clarity. Best use: standalone dirt. Closest to a "pedal that contains an SD-1 mode", but the single shared waveshaper can't switch circuit modes per the real Bonsai.

**`mod-caps-Spice.lv2` / `mod-caps-SpiceX2.lv2`** (CAPS Spice) — Spice uses two band-split shape stages; one of its saturator modes (`atan15`, mode 2 of Saturate) is **genuinely asymmetric** — it selects `atantbl` for positive and `one5tbl` for negative based on the sign bit (`polynomials.cc:1180`). Other modes (atan, clip, one5, tanh, pow7) are symmetric. Spice is a multi-band "not-an-exciter" saturation, not an overdrive pedal — feed-forward band-splitter → waveshaper → sum. Quality: medium (polynomial approximations). Best use: mix-bus / tone colour, not pedal dirt.

**`mod-caps-Saturate.lv2`** (CAPS Saturate) — Same waveshaper set as Spice, single-band. Mode 2 (`atan15`) asymmetric, all others symmetric. Best use: general saturation colour.

**`mod-mda-Overdrive.lv2`** (MDA Overdrive) — **SYMMETRIC.** `sign(x)*sqrt(|x|)` plus a one-pole filter (`mdaOverdrive.cpp:159`). Odd-symmetric. Generic soft overdrive. Quality: low (cheap waveshaper). Best use: light dirt colour.

**`mod-mda-Bandisto.lv2`** (MDA Multi-band Distortion) — `1/(1+d*|x|)` per band, **symmetric**, with an optional "valve" mode that zeroes the distortion on positive half-cycles → **one-sided asymmetric** (only the negative half is distorted). That's a feed-forward 3-band splitter, not a diode pedal. Best use: tone colour.

**`mod-ds1.lv2` / `dm-DS1.lv2`** (Dave Mollen's DS-1, Rust) — **SYMMETRIC.** The clipper (`clipper.rs:31`) is `x / (1+x⁴)^½` using `x.abs()`, fully odd-symmetric. Models the DS-1's op-amp + transistor booster + tone stack filters faithfully but **clips symmetrically** — the DS-1's real diode asymmetry is not modelled. Quality: high (proper circuit topology, oversampled). Tone: DS-1-ish, bright, edgy. Best use: standalone distortion. Not an SD-1 substitute and not even a faithful DS-1 (asymmetry-wise).

**`drowaudio-distortion.lv2` / `drowaudio-distortionshaper.lv2`** — Source not in the cloned repo (only the demo app is present); cannot characterise from source. Skip.

**`ZamAutoSat.lv2`** (Zam AutoSat) — **SYMMETRIC.** `2*x*(1-|x|/2)` (`ZamAutoSatPlugin.cpp:71`), odd-symmetric. Auto-saturation, no user control. Best use: subtle master-bus colour.

**`ZynDistortion.lv2`** (ZynAddSubFX Distortion) — Multi-mode waveshaper with **several genuinely asymmetric modes**: mode 2 "Asymmetric" `sin(x*(0.1+ws-ws*x))`, mode 12 "Asym2" `tmp*(1-tmp)*(tmp+2)` with bounds (-2, 1) → 2:1 asymmetric, mode 13 "Pow2" similarly, modes 8/9 one-sided limiters. All are **hand-drawn static waveshapers**, not diode-equation models. Default mode is user-selectable. Quality: medium (closed-form polynomials/sines with oversampling). Tone: highly mode-dependent, generic synth distortion. Best use: colour/special-effect, not pedal emulation.

**`MetalTone.lv2`** (brummer10 MetalTone, Boss MT-2 Metal Zone derivative) — **SYMMETRIC.** Single `metalzone_p2_table` with `copysign(f, x)` (`metalzone_p2_table.h:84`). The MT-2 is a multi-stage distortion with a parametric mid-scoop; the model captures the filter network but clips symmetrically. Best use: scooped high-gain standalone dirt.

**`XDarkTerror.lv2`, `XTinyTerror.lv2`** (brummer10 Orange amp models) — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC (tube).** Multiple `_neg_table`/`_table` pairs selected via `signbit` (e.g. `darkterror.cc:446`). Tube preamp models of the Orange Dark Terror / Tiny Terror. Asymmetry is tube-preamp, ~1.1–1.5:1. Wrong family (amp preamp). Best use: amp model.

**`FatFrog.lv2`** (brummer10 FatFrog, JMP/Plexi-style high-gain amp) — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC (tube).** `ba.if(fsignbit(_), fatfrog_p2_neg_clip, fatfrog_p2_clip)` (`FatFrog.dsp:237`). Preamp p2 stage: pos saturates -0.833, neg 0.550, ratio ~1.5:1. Power amp also asymmetric. Tube amp model. Best use: amp model.

**`LittleFly.lv2`** (brummer10 LittleFly) — **GENUINELY ASYMMETRIC (tube).** `littleflyclip` / `littlefly_neg_clip` via `fsignbit` (`LittleFly.dsp:184`). Single-stage tube preamp. Tube family. Best use: small amp-in-a-box.

**`VintageAC30.lv2`** (brummer10 Vox AC30) — **MILDLY ASYMMETRIC (tube).** Multiple `_neg_table`/`_table` pairs. Normal channel: pos -0.833, neg +0.716, ratio ~1.16:1. Power amp: pos 0.706, neg -0.709 (nearly symmetric). Tube amp model. Best use: amp model.

**`SoulForce.lv2`, `profiler.lv2`** — Not findable as separate repos on GitHub; likely `dpf-plugins-labs` SoulForce (a DPF labs plugin). Could not clone the DSP source; skip.

## Recommendation

If the editorial needs a plugin that **genuinely produces the SD-1's even-harmonic asymmetric-diode feel**:

1. **Best available: `gx_bossds1.lv2`** — the only plugin in the set with a faithful diode-equation asymmetric soft+hard clipper modelled from a real Boss pedal. It's a DS-1, not an SD-1 (hard-clip-to-ground vs feedback soft clip; brighter, edgier), but it's the same manufacturer's family with real diode asymmetry, and the asymmetry ratio (~1.2:1) is in the right ballpark for a Boss pedal.
2. **Best asymmetry-ratio match: `gx_bajatubedriver.lv2`** — its 1:2.02 clip-threshold ratio is almost exactly the SD-1's, but the circuit is a tube driver, not a diode-feedback overdrive, so the *character* of the asymmetry is valve-soft, not diode-edgy.
3. **Best circuit-family match: `gx_eternitysim.lv2`** — op-amp feedback soft clip + mid-hump + low compression, the right overdrive family, but it's a Lovepedal Eternity (Klon-adjacent) with the *inverse* asymmetry ratio of the SD-1.

**The real SD-1 is simply not modelled on the pi-Stomp image.** The plugin named after it (`gx_sd1sim.lv2`) is a confirmed impostor (symmetric `copysign(f, -x)`).
